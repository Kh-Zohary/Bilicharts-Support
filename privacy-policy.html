Bilirubin charts Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 01, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bilirubin charts handles information when you use the Bilirubin charts mobile application.

Information we collect

Bilirubin charts does not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, phone number, or other identifying information to use the app.

When you use premium subscription features, subscription-related information may be processed by our third-party subscription service provider, RevenueCat, and by Apple or Google as applicable. This may include an app-specific user identifier, purchase history, and information about interactions with subscription-related features. This information is used to provide and manage subscriptions and premium access.

Information entered into the app

Bilirubin charts is designed to perform clinical calculations on the device. Information entered into calculation fields is not intentionally collected by Bilirubin charts for advertising or user profiling.

How information is used

Information processed in connection with subscriptions is used to provide app functionality, manage premium access, process purchases, and maintain subscription status.

Advertising and sale of information

Bilirubin charts does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

Third-party services

Bilirubin charts uses RevenueCat to help manage in-app subscriptions. Apple App Store and Google Play may also process purchase and subscription information according to their respective privacy policies.

Data retention

Subscription-related information may be retained by the relevant service providers for as long as necessary to provide their services, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Children's privacy

Bilirubin charts is a professional clinical tool and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
